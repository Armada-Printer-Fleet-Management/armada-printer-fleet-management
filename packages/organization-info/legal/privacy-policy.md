# Privacy Policy

PLACEHOLDER. This policy is a stub, so the agreement gate has something to ask for. It is not
legal advice and describes no real practice. Each deployment replaces this file with its own
policy, then updates its effective date and SHA-256 in organization.json.

1. The software stores your account, your print jobs and the files you upload.
2. The organization that runs it decides who can see them and how long they are kept.
3. Ask that organization about your data.
