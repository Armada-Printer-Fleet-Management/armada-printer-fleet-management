Git hygiene guide

How a change travels from your branch to production, and why the history is kept the way it is. It describes what to do most of the time, not a rigid requirement: when you depart from it, say why in the pull request. The exact rules, branch names and commands are under Branching in AGENTS.md, and agents can do this plumbing for you.

In short

  • Rebase, never merge main in. History stays one straight line, and your branch is always tested on top of the newest code, so nothing changes underneath you after review.
  • One ticket, one commit. Every line of history leads to one ticket, which is where the decision behind it is recorded.
  • The commit message is checked. A hook rejects a message in the wrong shape; /commit-message writes one for you.
  • Production only gets small, proven fixes. Anything else waits for the next version.
